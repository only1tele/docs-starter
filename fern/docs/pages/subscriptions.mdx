## Plans

A plan is the product: an amount, a currency, and how often it is charged.

```bash title="Create a plan"
curl --request POST \
  --url https://api.mitu.ai/v1/subscription-plans \
  --header "x-api-key: $MITU_API_KEY" \
  --header "Content-Type: application/json" \
  --data '{
    "name": "Pro",
    "description": "Unlimited lookups and priority support",
    "amount": 49.00,
    "currency": "USD",
    "billingInterval": "month",
    "features": ["Unlimited lookups", "Priority support"]
  }'
```

<ParamField path="name" type="string" required={true}>
  What the payer sees.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="amount" type="number" required={true}>
  Charged once per interval.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="currency" type="string" required={true} />

<ParamField path="billingInterval" type="day | week | month | year" required={true}>
  How often the charge recurs.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="features" type="array">
  Strings shown alongside the plan.
</ParamField>

A plan is `active`, `paused` or `archived`.

## Subscriptions

```bash title="Subscribe someone to a plan"
curl --request POST \
  --url https://api.mitu.ai/v1/subscriptions \
  --header "x-api-key: $MITU_API_KEY" \
  --header "Content-Type: application/json" \
  --data '{
    "subscriptionPlanId": "plan_7h2kq9xz",
    "paymentMethod": "mpp_crypto",
    "subscriberId": "cus_ds9hazr8jd3l",
    "subscriberType": "user"
  }'
```

<ParamField path="subscriptionPlanId" type="string" required={true} />

<ParamField path="paymentMethod" type="mpp_crypto | mpp_spt" required={true}>
  How the recurring charge settles.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="subscriberId" type="string">
  The agent or user that subscribed.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="subscriberType" type="agent | user" default="agent" />

<Warning title="Recurring charges settle over MPP only">
`x402` is a one-time authorisation signed for a specific amount and recipient, so it cannot carry a future charge. A subscription takes `mpp_crypto` or `mpp_spt`, and passing `x402` is rejected.
</Warning>

A subscription is `active`, `past_due`, `paused`, `cancelled` or `expired`.

```text
POST /v1/subscriptions/{id}/cancel    stop it
GET  /v1/subscriptions/{id}/invoices  what it has generated
```

Each charge produces an invoice, itself `pending`, `paid`, `failed`, `refunded` or `void`.

## Offering plans through a link

A payment link of type `subscription` carries a `planId`, or several through `planIds` with `allowCustomerChoice`, which lets the payer pick. Amount and currency come from the plan rather than from the link. See [payment links](/platform/payments/payment-experiences).

## Events

`plan.created`, `subscription.created`, `subscription.charged`, `subscription.past_due` and `subscription.cancelled` are all emitted. [Events](/platform/webhooks/events) covers delivery.
