---
title: OAuth
subtitle: What happens to the customer record when somebody signs in with your own identity provider.
---

With customer sign-in enabled, you do not create customers in advance. The record is made the first time somebody signs in.

```text
Customer adds your MCP URL to their client
        |
Client discovers the OAuth configuration
        |
Customer signs in with your identity provider
        |
Mitu receives the identity
        |
Mitu creates or maps the customer from sub
        |
customer.created
        |
You can now act on that customer, including issuing a wallet
```

## The record that results

```json title="customer.created data"
{
  "source": "oauth",
  "client_reference_id": "<oauth-sub>"
}
```

`client_reference_id` is populated from the identity provider's `sub`, so the Mitu customer maps back to the same user your own system knows. Store the pairing of that `sub` and the Mitu customer id if you will later act on the customer from your backend.

## What this does not do

<Note title="Signing in does not create a wallet">
A wallet exists only because you created one through the API. OAuth sign-in creates a customer and nothing more. Issuing a wallet remains an explicit merchant action, because a wallet costs real addresses to fund and splits a customer's money across pots.
</Note>

No prior `POST /v1/customers` call is required or expected in this flow. Creating the customer yourself and then having the same person sign in through OAuth produces two records unless the `client_reference_id` you used already matches their `sub`.
