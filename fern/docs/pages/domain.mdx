## Integration

Two settings decide what Mitu does with a call once an agent makes it.

```text
API base URL        https://api.yoursite.com
Health check path   polled, so the dashboard can show whether your API is up
Customer sign-in    off, or your own OAuth
```

You give Mitu no credential for your API. Every forwarded request is signed with a secret Mitu issues to you, and that signature is what proves a call is genuine. See [request signature verification](/platform/security/request-verification).

Turn customer sign-in on and your customers authenticate with the credentials they already have. Mitu forwards their access token with every request, so your existing endpoint keeps working unchanged. [MCP authentication](/platform/mcp/authentication) covers both methods.

## Your own domain

By default agents reach you at a Mitu URL. Claim your own and they reach you at yours instead:

```text
https://mcp.yourdomain.com/mcp
```

<Steps>

<Step title="Add the domain">
Enter the hostname you want to use on the **Domain** page.
</Step>

<Step title="Add the DNS records">
Mitu shows the records to create. Add them with your DNS provider exactly as given.
</Step>

<Step title="Verify ownership">
Ownership is proved by a TXT lookup. Verification can be retried while DNS propagates.
</Step>

<Step title="Wait for the certificate">
The certificate is issued after verification succeeds.
</Step>

<Step title="Use the new URL">
Give customers the custom URL. Until verification and the certificate are both done, the Mitu URL keeps working, so nothing breaks while you wait.
</Step>

</Steps>
