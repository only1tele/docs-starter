---
title: Settlement
subtitle: What happens to money after it is paid to you.
---

Everything you receive lands in your Mitu balance as USDC, whoever paid it and however they paid. A card payment through `mpp_spt` and an on-chain settlement through x402 arrive in the same place.

From there, two ways out:

**Settle in local currency.** Converted and paid to your bank account.

**Withdraw the stablecoin.** Sent on-chain to an address you control.

     Caption: "Settle received funds to your bank account or withdraw

## Payer method and payout destination are separate

How somebody paid you has no bearing on how you take the money out. You do not need a wallet to accept on-chain payments, and accepting cards does not tie you to a bank payout. The balance is the join between the two halves.

## From the dashboard

Payouts are a dashboard action, taken by a person signed in to Mitu. There is no payout endpoint in the public API, and that is deliberate: an API key that could move your balance to an arbitrary address would be a key worth stealing.

<Note title="settlement.completed is not emitted yet">
A `settlement.completed` event type exists, but nothing in the current implementation raises it. Do not build a reconciliation flow that waits for it. Reconcile against `payment.completed`, which is emitted for every payment you receive.
</Note>
