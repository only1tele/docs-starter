<h1 className="mitu-title">Mitu Documentation</h1>

<p className="mitu-p">Mitu gives businesses the infrastructure to serve both humans and AI agents. Bring your existing products, services and APIs to AI agents, accept agent payments, and settle funds to your bank account or onchain address.</p>

<p className="mitu-p">Mitu also provides Agent Wallets, which let you give your customers wallets their AI agents can spend from, with configurable spending limits and approval controls. These wallets can be funded with stablecoins or local bank transfers in NGN (bank transfer), USD (ACH, FedNow, Wire), EUR (SEPA), GBP (Faster Payments), MXN (SPEI), BRL (Pix), and COP (Bre-B and bank transfer).</p>

<p className="mitu-p">Beyond Agent Wallets and agent payments, you can create payment links, invoices, subscriptions, and other payment experiences for humans and AI agents through Mitu’s APIs.</p>
