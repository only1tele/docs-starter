---
title: Invoices
subtitle: Create and manage invoices with line items, due dates and payment experiences for people and AI agents.
---

Use invoices when you need a formal billing record rather than a simple one-time payment. An invoice can contain multiple line items, tax, discounts, a due date and your own invoice number, while giving you a lifecycle to track from creation through payment.

Invoices are addressed to customers and can be paid by either a person or an AI agent. Both payer experiences belong to the same invoice, so your billing record and reconciliation remain consistent regardless of how payment is completed.

## Create an invoice

Create an invoice for the customer you want to bill and provide at least one line item.

```bash title="Create an invoice"
curl --request POST \
  --url https://api.mitu.ai/v1/invoices \
  --header "x-api-key: $MITU_API_KEY" \
  --header "Content-Type: application/json" \
  --data '{
    "customerId": "cus_ds9hazr8jd3l",
    "invoiceNumber": "INV-2026-441",
    "description": "Design services, September",
    "currency": "USD",
    "lineItems": [
      {
        "name": "Design retainer",
        "quantity": 1,
        "amount": 1200.00
      },
      {
        "name": "Additional revisions",
        "description": "3 rounds",
        "quantity": 3,
        "amount": 150.00
      }
    ],
    "taxAmount": 123.75,
    "discountAmount": 0,
    "dueAt": "2026-10-01T00:00:00Z"
  }'
```

### Parameters

| Parameter | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `customerId` | `string` · required | The customer the invoice belongs to. The customer must already exist before you create the invoice. See [Customers](/platform/customers/customer-api). |
| `lineItems` | `array` · required | The products, services or other charges included on the invoice. Provide at least one line item. Each contains `name`, `quantity` and `amount`, with an optional `description`. |
| `currency` | `string` · required | The currency used for the invoice and its line items. |
| `invoiceNumber` | `string` | Your own invoice number. If you do not provide one, a number is assigned for you. |
| `description` | `string` | Additional context describing what the invoice is for. |
| `taxAmount` | `number` | The tax added to the invoice total. The default is `0`. |
| `discountAmount` | `number` | The discount deducted from the invoice total. The default is `0`. |
| `dueAt` | `string` | The date and time the invoice is due, provided as an ISO 8601 timestamp. |

## Invoice lifecycle

Creating an invoice does not immediately send it to the customer. A new invoice starts as a draft, giving you an opportunity to review or amend the billing information before making it payable.

When the invoice is ready, send it to the customer.

```http
POST /v1/invoices/{id}/send
```

You can retrieve and manage invoices through the API throughout their lifecycle.

```http
GET    /v1/invoices
GET    /v1/invoices/{id}
PATCH  /v1/invoices/{id}
DELETE /v1/invoices/{id}/cancel
```

Keep invoice state in your own system in sync with the API and webhook events rather than relying on what the payer sees in their payment experience.

## Getting an invoice paid

A sent invoice can be completed by either a person or an AI agent. The invoice provides the appropriate payment entry point for each payer while keeping both attached to the same underlying invoice.

A person opens the hosted invoice payment experience and completes the payment through the available methods. An AI agent uses the programmatic invoice payment URL and completes the payment through the agent payment flow.

Use the URLs returned for the invoice rather than constructing them yourself. This keeps your integration independent of how invoice payment URLs are structured.

<Note title="No separate invoice for an AI agent">
  You do not need to create another invoice when an AI agent is paying on behalf of the customer. The same invoice can support both human and agent payment experiences.
</Note>

See [Payment experiences](/platform/payments/payment-experiences) for how human and agent payment entry points work, and [Agent payments](/platform/payments/agent-payments) for the agent payment flow.

## Track invoice events

Use webhook events to keep the invoice state in your application up to date. The invoice lifecycle can produce events when an invoice is created, sent and paid.

```text
invoice.created
invoice.sent
invoice.paid
```

When payment is received, `payment.completed` is also emitted for the underlying payment. Use the invoice information in the event to associate the payment with the correct billing record.

Do not update invoice state based only on a browser redirect or information reported by an AI client. Process verified webhook events or retrieve the current invoice state through the API.

<Note title="Events not yet emitted">
  `invoice.viewed` and `invoice.overdue` are declared event types but are not currently emitted. Do not depend on them in production until they are supported.

  If you need to determine whether an invoice is overdue, compare `dueAt` with the current invoice payment state in your own system.
</Note>

See [Events](/platform/webhooks/events) for invoice and payment event payloads.

## Reconcile invoice payments

Keep your own invoice number or business reference associated with the invoice so your application can reconcile payments against records it already understands.

An invoice being paid and the underlying service being delivered are separate business states. When payment completes, update the billing record and then continue whatever fulfilment process applies to the invoice in your own system.

This is especially important when invoice payment triggers another action, such as activating a service, releasing an order or confirming a booking.
